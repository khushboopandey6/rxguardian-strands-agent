Protecting Underserved Patients from Preventable Medication Harm

Over 1.5 million people are harmed every year by preventable prescription errors, with elderly, ESL, and low-literacy patients at highest risk. RxGuardian uses a 5-agent Strands graph to screen dangerous drug clashes, simplify instructions to a 5th-grade reading level, and generate clear daily routines.
